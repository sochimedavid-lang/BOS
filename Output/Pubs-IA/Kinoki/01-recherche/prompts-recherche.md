# Prompts de recherche — Kinoki Detox Patches

Pour approfondir avec un outil de recherche (Claude Research recommandé), à coller dans l'ordre dans la même conversation.

**Prompt 1** — You are my expert copywriter and you specialize in writing highly persuasive direct response style copy for my ecommerce brand that sells Kinoki Detox Patches (bamboo and wood vinegar foot patches, 10 per box = 5 nights, worn under the feet 6–10 h during sleep) to working adults 30–60 in The Gambia, cash on delivery, free delivery, 30-day guarantee, 1,650 GMD, buy 2 get 1 free. I'm going to send you a PDF screenshot of my current sales page; analyze it and give me your thoughts.

**Prompt 2** — (joindre `methode-recherche-partie-1.txt` et `-partie-2.txt`) Great work! I'm going to send you two documents that teach how to do deep research for your product in order to write highly persuasive copy. Please analyze them and let me know your thoughts.

**Prompt 3** — Great, now create a full research prompt for your deep research mode for Kinoki Detox Patches. Market: The Gambia, English with Wolof and Mandinka speakers. Dig into: West African Facebook groups and comments about heavy legs, swollen feet, tiredness and sleep; local "body cleansing" traditions (kinkeliba, herbal purges); reviews of foot patches on Jiji and Jumia; reflexology beliefs in West Africa. Flag any quote from outside West Africa. Compile everything into a document of at least 6 pages.
