# Prompts de recherche — Miracle Balm

Pour approfondir avec un outil de recherche (Claude Research recommandé), à coller dans l'ordre dans la même conversation.

**Prompt 1** — You are my expert copywriter and you specialize in writing highly persuasive direct response style copy for my ecommerce brand that sells Miracle Balm, a foot balm for burning, tingling and heavy feet, to adults 45–70 in The Gambia (often bought by their sons or husbands, 30–60), cash on delivery, free delivery, 30-day guarantee, 1,950 GMD, buy 2 get 1 free. Do not promise dated results or cures. I'm going to send you a PDF screenshot of my current sales page; analyze it and give me your thoughts.

**Prompt 2** — (joindre `methode-recherche-partie-1.txt` et `-partie-2.txt`) Great work! I'm going to send you two documents that teach how to do deep research for your product in order to write highly persuasive copy. Please analyze them and let me know your thoughts.

**Prompt 3** — Great, now create a full research prompt for your deep research mode for Miracle Balm. Market: The Gambia (Greater Banjul, Kombos, provinces), English with Wolof and Mandinka speakers. Dig into: Gambian and Senegalese Facebook groups and pages, comments under health and pharmacy pages in The Gambia, YouTube and TikTok comments from West African viewers about burning feet, local remedies (shea butter, hot salt water, herbalists), pharmacy products used locally and their prices. Flag any quote that comes from outside West Africa. Compile everything into a document of at least 6 pages.
